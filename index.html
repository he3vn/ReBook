Re:Zero, Starting Life in Another World

Re:Zero

Re:Zero, Starting Life in Another World follows Subaru Natsuki, pulled out of a convenience store and into a world of spirits, swords and witches. He has one power: when he dies, the clock turns back, and he keeps every death in his head. Tappei Nagatsuki's light novel series runs to forty-six volumes, and this edition publishes the whole run in order — the returns, the witches, and everyone Subaru refuses to lose.
