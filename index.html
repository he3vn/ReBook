Re:Zero

Re:Zero is truly a masterpiece. It's the best piece of Japanese fiction,everything from its amazing world and story building, to its intricate dialogues, amazing main characters, sidecast, and team consisting of voice actors passionately voicing the best lines and an animation team who captures every frame like I like them to. It really touched my heart and changed my life. I want ALL of you watching this site to read Re:Zero, so go do it!
